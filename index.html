About

ClipFetch is a simple browser-based tool for trying to download directly accessible video files. It does not host videos or bypass platform restrictions.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my social media link not downloading?

Many social media links point to a webpage rather than a video file. Those links require a compatible service and permission to access the media.

Can I choose HD or 4K quality?

This HTML-only version cannot choose resolutions that the source does not expose as downloadable files.

Is every video supported?

No. Access rules, CORS, login requirements, and copyright restrictions may prevent downloading.

Privacy

This page has no built-in account system. When you submit a URL, your browser attempts to access that URL. External websites may apply their own privacy policies and access rules.
